\section{\href{https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.93.022703}{Quantum chaos in 
ultracold collisions between Yb(\textsuperscript{1}S\textsubscript{0}) and 
Yb(\textsuperscript{3}P\textsubscript{2})}}

 \subsection*{Supervisor: Prof. Dermot Green}


We calculate and analyze Feshbach resonance spectra for ultracold 
Yb(\textsuperscript{1}S\textsubscript{0})+ Yb(\textsuperscript{3}P\textsubscript{2}) collisions as a function of an interatomic 
potential scaling factor $\lambda$ and external magnetic field. We show that, at zero 
field, the resonances are distributed randomly in $\lambda$, but that 
signatures of quantum chaos emerge as a field is applied. The random 
zero-field distribution arises from superposition of structured spectra 
associated with individual total angular momenta. In addition, we show that 
the resonances with respect to magnetic field in the experimentally accessible 
range of 400 to 2000 G are chaotically distributed, with strong level repulsion 
that is characteristic of quantum chaos.
