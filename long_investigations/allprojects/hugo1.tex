\section{\href{https://doi.org/10.1103/rwhj-df31}{Impact of electron 
correlation on photoelectron angular distributions in sequential double 
photoionization of Ne\textsuperscript{+}}}

\subsection*{Supervisor: Prof. Hugo van der Hart}



We present a combination of the R-matrix theory with time dependence (RMT) 
method with density matrix theory to model sequential ionization processes. 
Separate RMT calculations model the individual ionization stages, while a 
density matrix describes the coherences within the intermediate ion. As proof 
of concept, we quantify changes in the angular distribution of photoelectrons 
in the final stage of sequential ionization of Ne\textsuperscript{+}
to Ne\textsuperscript{3+} by x-ray free-electron laser radiation with photon 
energies of 95 eV.
